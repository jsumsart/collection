Object Record

Sunset

Kojin Toneyama

Accession Number JSUART-1966-003
Date 1966
Medium Lithograph in color
Dimensions 17 x 21.75 in

A boldly abstract lithograph on paper, dominated by a vibrant yellow ground. Across the surface, thick black brush–like strokes swirl into a loose horizon line. Spontaneous splatters of red and gestural grays animate the mid‑ground, giving the work a dynamic, almost musical energy.
