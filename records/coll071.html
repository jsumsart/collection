Description

Object description

A vibrant portrait of a young Black man at his easel. He sits front‑center, holding a green‑edged canvas. He wears a striped collared shirt beneath a rust‑brown sweater vest. Warm studio lighting highlights his rich brown skin tones and the colorful geometric shapes of his workspace behind him.
