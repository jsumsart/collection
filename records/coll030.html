Description

Object description

A minimalistic winter landscape with a barren tree at the center. The subdued color palette and delicate brushstrokes create a tranquil and contemplative mood, emphasizing nature’s quiet beauty.

About the Work

Collection context

Abstract Landscape by Anderson Macklin is part of the Jackson State University Department of Art Permanent Collection. Research into the work, its context, and its collection history is ongoing.
