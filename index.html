About

A teaching collection, a local archive, and a public record of artistic life

The Jackson State University Department of Art Permanent Collection was shaped by faculty practice, student work, visiting artists, gifts, exhibitions, and long-term acts of preservation. This site treats the collection as both an art-historical resource and a documentary record of institutional memory at an HBCU.

Catalog Practice

Structured for research, not only display

Records expose object numbers, creator attributions, dates, rights statements, and source fields so the public interface behaves more like a museum catalog than a promotional gallery.

Interpretation

Grounded in collection files and catalog documentation

Descriptions and published notes are derived from the working catalog and supporting local documentation, with the understanding that collection knowledge remains active and revisable.

Public Use

Built for browsing, citation, and stewardship

Stable record pages support sharing and citation, while the CSV and table view make the collection easier to inspect as a body of records rather than only as isolated images.

Stewardship

Rights, access, and record quality

Rights statements published here are drawn from the collection metadata and should be read as the governing public guidance for each record. Metadata remains under active review, and this site should be understood as a public access catalog for an actively documented departmental collection rather than a finished scholarly catalog raisonne.

For permissions, corrections, object information, or research inquiries, contact the Jackson State University Department of Art. Stable record pages on this site are intended to support discovery, teaching, and responsible public access.
