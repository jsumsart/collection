Jackson State University

Art collection, public memory, and institutional record.

The JSU Department of Art Permanent Collection gathers artworks, teaching materials, portraits, prints, and records of artistic life connected to Jackson State University. This site is designed as a public-facing collection interface for research, teaching, institutional memory, and object-level discovery.

Browse the collection Read about the collection

Collection Focus

This site presents the collection as a body of records: objects, creators, subjects, places, and documentation that together trace the life of the department.

Use the Site

Start with the browse page for records, images, and metadata. Use the about page for institutional context, stewardship framing, and collection history.

For permissions, corrections, object information, or research inquiries, contact the Jackson State University Department of Art. This public site is designed as an active collection record.
