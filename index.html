About

A teaching collection, an archive, and a record of Black artistic formation

The Jackson State University Department of Art Permanent Collection was formed through the daily working life of an art department: classrooms, studios, exhibitions, visiting artists, gifts, workshops, and acts of preservation. It reflects the labor of faculty, students, alumni, and community networks who built a visual arts program within the context of an HBCU in Mississippi.

Collection History

More than a conventional museum collection

The collection includes paintings, prints, ceramics, photographs, works on paper, portraits, graphic works, faculty work, student studies, international prints, and South African works. Its unevenness is part of its meaning: attributed works, unknown makers, fragmentary records, and vulnerable objects all speak to the realities of collection stewardship under unequal conditions.

Institutional Context

Rooted in Jackson, connected far beyond it

Catalog research traces connections among Johnson Hall, Jackson State University, Tougaloo, Memphis, the Mississippi Delta, Black Studies, and broader diasporic and international visual traditions. The collection documents not only artworks, but also the institutional, civic, and pedagogical worlds that shaped them.

Documentation

Grounded in the catalog and local research files

This site draws on local catalog documentation, figure caption audits, and metadata review files assembled for the JSU permanent collection. Public-facing copy here is adapted from those materials to support browsing, teaching, and further research.

Stewardship

Rights, use, and research

Artwork records on this site retain the rights statements supplied with the collection metadata. Reproduction, redistribution, and publication may require written permission from the Jackson State University Department of Art. Researchers should treat this site as a public access layer for an actively documented teaching collection rather than a final scholarly catalogue raisonne.

For permissions, collection questions, or research inquiries, contact the Jackson State University Department of Art. This site publishes selected records and interpretive context from an actively documented departmental collection.
