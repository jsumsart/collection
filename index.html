Jackson State University

Art, public memory, and the institutional record.

The Jackson State University Department of Art Permanent Collection preserves artworks, teaching materials, prints, portraits, and other records of artistic life connected to the university. This digital database makes the collection accessible for research, teaching, and public discovery while documenting the artists, educators, students, exhibitions, and communities that have shaped the Department of Art across generations.

About this digital resource

This database is an evolving record of the Jackson State University Department of Art Permanent Collection. Collection information will continue to be expanded and revised as new research, documentation, and object histories become available. For permissions, corrections, additional object information, or research inquiries, contact the Jackson State University Department of Art.
