Jackson State University

Art collection, public memory, and object records

The JSU Department of Art Permanent Collection brings together artworks, teaching materials, portraits, prints, and records of artistic life connected to Jackson State University. This site combines museum-style browsing with stable object pages for research, teaching, and stewardship.

Public Access

A cleaner front door to the collection

The home page now stays focused on orientation, institutional framing, and clear pathways into the collection. Visitors who want to see artworks and records in depth move directly into the browse experience, while the About page carries the fuller historical and stewardship context.

For permissions, corrections, object information, or research inquiries, contact the Jackson State University Department of Art. This public site is designed as an active collection record.
