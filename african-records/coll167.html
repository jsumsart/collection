African Art Record

Lega-Style Oval Face Mask

Lega or related eastern Congo peoples, attribution tentative

Object Number JA-2022-18
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Lega or related eastern Congo peoples, attribution tentative
Object Type Face mask / Lega-style mask

This carved wooden oval mask has a pale worn surface, long central ridge or nose, small slit eyes, incised hairline or brow band, small mouth, and dark border. The restrained abstract features relate to Lega and related eastern Congolese mask traditions. Its weathered surface and reduced face create a quiet, meditative presence.
