African Art Record

Namji-Style Fertility Doll with Beaded Face

Namji/Namchi or Dowayo peoples, northern Cameroon, attribution tentative

Object Number JA-2022-26
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Namji/Namchi or Dowayo peoples, northern Cameroon, attribution tentative
Object Type Fertility doll / decorated figural doll

This carved wooden standing doll-like figure has a large rounded head, a bead row running vertically down the face, raised eyes, triangular light-colored mouth element, elongated torso, arms held close to the body, bent legs, and worn dark patina. The geometric form and bead or metal embellishment relate to Namji, Namchi, or Dowayo fertility dolls from northern Cameroon. Its compact body and ornamented face create a striking personal object.
