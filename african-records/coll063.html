African Art Record

Teke-Style Standing Figure with Openwork Torso

Teke-style, attribution uncertain

Object Number JA-2014-07
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Teke-style, attribution uncertain
Object Type Standing figure / possible power or ancestor figure

This Teke-style standing figure has elongated proportions, pierced eyes, a cylindrical neck with beaded collar, metal ring earrings, and openwork lozenge-shaped cutouts through the torso. The ornaments frame the figure as both body and object of display. Its openwork center gives the compact sculpture an unusual architectural quality.
