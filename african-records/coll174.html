African Art Record

Seated Mother-and-Child Figure

Central African peoples, possibly Chokwe or related region, attribution uncertain

Object Number JA-2022-25
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Central African peoples, possibly Chokwe or related region, attribution uncertain
Object Type Mother-and-child figure / maternity figure

This seated female figure holds or nurses a small child at the front of the torso. The figure has a rounded head with cap-like coiffure or head covering, incised hairline, calm features, bent arms, seated pose, and circular base. The mother-and-child subject emphasizes nurture, continuity, and family or lineage connection.
