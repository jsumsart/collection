African Art Record

Grassfields-Style Button-Covered Masquerade Headdress

Cameroon Grassfields-style, possibly Bamileke, attribution uncertain

Object Number JA-2022-40
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Cameroon Grassfields-style, possibly Bamileke, attribution uncertain
Object Type Masquerade headdress / costume element

This conical fiber or textile headdress is densely covered with sewn buttons and topped with bound twig-like projections. The form relates to masquerade and prestige costume elements from the Cameroon Grassfields region, including Bamileke-related traditions. The buttoned surface catches light in a way that recalls the visual richness of beadwork.
