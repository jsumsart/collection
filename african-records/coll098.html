African Art Record

Yaka- or Suku-Style Initiation Mask (Ndeemba/Kholuka Type)

Yaka or Suku-style, attribution probable

Object Number JA-2018-02
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Yaka or Suku-style, attribution probable
Object Type Initiation mask / helmet or face mask with raffia collar

This Yaka or Suku-related initiation mask has a white-painted wooden face, square eye openings, projecting central nose ridge, small mouth, vertical nasal detail, dark cap or crown, and large raffia collar. The form parallels Ndeemba or Kholuka masks associated with mukanda or n-khanda initiation contexts in southwestern Democratic Republic of the Congo. The raffia collar would have animated the mask in performance.
