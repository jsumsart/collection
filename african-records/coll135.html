African Art Record

Makonde-Style Ujamaa or Tree-of-Life Figural Sculpture

Makonde-style, attribution uncertain

Object Number TC-2015-23
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Makonde-style, attribution uncertain
Object Type Ujamaa / Tree of Life figural sculpture

This carved wooden sculpture is composed of multiple interlocking human figures rising from a shared base. The repeated elongated bodies and heads form a clustered vertical composition. The work resembles Makonde ujamaa, or Tree of Life, carving traditions from Tanzania and Mozambique, where linked figures can suggest kinship, community, and continuity.
