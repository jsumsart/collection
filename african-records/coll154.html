African Art Record

Fang Ngil-Style White Face Mask

Fang Ngil-style, attribution uncertain

Object Number JA-2022-05
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Fang Ngil-style, attribution uncertain
Object Type White face mask / masquerade mask

This carved wooden face mask has a pale white surface, elongated oval face, small slit eyes, long narrow nose, and open mouth. The white pigment and elongated facial type relate to Fang Ngil masks from Gabon and neighboring areas. Its spare features create a solemn, elongated presence.
