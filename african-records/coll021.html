African Art Record

Kuba-Style Prestige Belt with Beads and Cowries

Kuba-style or closely related Kasai-region group, attribution probable

Object Number JA-2011-08
Date 20th century
Culture / Community Kuba-style or closely related Kasai-region group, attribution probable
Object Type Prestige belt / beaded belt / body adornment

This Kuba-style prestige belt is made from a fiber backing densely decorated with glass beads and rows of cowrie shells in red, black, and white geometric patterns. Cowries and beads were valued materials in Kuba courtly and prestige arts. The repeated motifs and rich surface announce status through both pattern and material abundance.
