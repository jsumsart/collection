African Art Record

Cowrie-Decorated Helmet Mask or Headdress

Unidentified West or Central African peoples

Object Number TC-2015-09
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Unidentified West or Central African peoples
Object Type Cowrie-decorated helmet mask / headdress

This carved wooden helmet-mask form has a rounded upper head, a front panel densely covered with rows of cowrie shells or shell-like rings, and lower vertical grooves suggesting a stylized mouth, beard, or pendant elements. Cowrie decoration appears in many West and Central African prestige and masquerade contexts. The shell-covered surface creates a rich visual texture associated with value and display.
