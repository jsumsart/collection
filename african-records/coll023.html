Hemba-Style Kneeling Female Ancestor Figure

Hemba-style, attribution uncertain

Object Number JA-2011-11
Date 20th century
Culture / Community Hemba-style, attribution uncertain
Object Type Kneeling female figure / possible ancestor figure

This small kneeling female figure has a large rounded head, closed eyes, elongated neck, pronounced navel, compact body, and polished surface. The raised hands and composed face create a pose of attention or inward focus. Its proportions relate to Hemba and neighboring eastern Congolese figural traditions.
