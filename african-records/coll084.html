African Art Record

Dan Ceremonial Spoon or Feast Ladle (Wunkirmian/Wakemia Type)

Dan peoples

Object Number JA-2017-12
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Dan peoples
Object Type Ceremonial spoon / feast ladle / wunkirmian or wakemia type

This Dan ceremonial spoon or feast ladle has a large oval bowl and a long handle surmounted by a stylized female head with incised coiffure and facial features. Dan spoons, often called wunkirmian or wakemia, are prestige objects associated with hospitality, generosity, and women honored for feast-making roles. The object joins utility, sculpture, and social recognition.
