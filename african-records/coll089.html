African Art Record

Oval Face Mask with Red Eye Accents

Unidentified Central or West-Central African peoples

Object Number JA-2017-08
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Unidentified Central or West-Central African peoples
Object Type Face mask / masquerade mask

This oval face mask has a rounded forehead, oval eyes edged with reddish pigment, a long straight nose, open oval mouth, dark surface, and vertical red and light markings down the face. The mask's strong central axis and painted accents create a visually direct expression. Its form relates broadly to Central or West-Central African masking traditions.
