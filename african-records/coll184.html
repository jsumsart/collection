African Art Record

Fish-Form Carved Wooden Object

Unidentified West or Central African peoples

Object Number JA-2022-35
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Unidentified West or Central African peoples
Object Type Fish-form carved object

This elongated carved wooden object takes the form of a fish or aquatic animal, with a tapered tail or blade-like extension, rounded head, small inlaid or painted eye, and worn brown surface. Fish-form carving and aquatic imagery appear in multiple West and Central African traditions. The streamlined body gives the object a sense of movement.
