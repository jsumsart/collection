African Art Record

Painted Oval Face Mask

West or Central African peoples, attribution uncertain

Object Number JA-2022-12
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community West or Central African peoples, attribution uncertain
Object Type Painted face mask / masquerade mask

This painted wooden face mask has slit eyes, a long nose, open mouth, and red, black, pale, and blue-green linear decoration. The painted bands enliven the oval face and emphasize its vertical structure. The mask is presented here as a visually distinctive masquerade face with a broad Central or West-Central African resonance.
