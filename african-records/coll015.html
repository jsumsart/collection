Hemba- or Luba-Hemba-Style Male Ancestor Figure

Hemba or Luba-Hemba-style, attribution probable

Object Number JA-2011-02
Date 20th century
Culture / Community Hemba or Luba-Hemba-style, attribution probable
Object Type Male ancestor figure / standing figure

This standing male figure is carved with elongated proportions, a calm face, closed almond-shaped eyes, patterned beard or jawline band, hands resting at the abdomen, and pronounced genitalia. The posture and restrained expression relate to Hemba and Luba-Hemba ancestor figure traditions. Its composed stance suggests dignity, continuity, and lineage memory.
