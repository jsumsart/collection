African Art Record

Songye Kifwebe-Style Mask with Raffia and Feathers

Songye-style, attribution probable

Object Number JA-2006-05
Date late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Songye-style, attribution probable
Object Type Face mask / kifwebe-style mask

This Songye-style kifwebe mask is carved with an elongated striated face, projecting mouth, plant-fiber beard, and feather elements. Kifwebe masks are associated with Songye and northern Luba masquerade traditions in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. The rhythmic grooves and projecting facial forms create a sense of disciplined movement and authority.
