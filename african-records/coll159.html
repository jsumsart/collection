African Art Record

Baule-Style Standing Female Figure

Baule-style, attribution uncertain

Object Number JA-2022-10
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Baule-style, attribution uncertain
Object Type Standing female figure / figural sculpture

This standing female figure has an oval face, high coiffure, long neck, pendant breasts, bent arms, compact legs, and a small base. The balanced pose and idealized body relate to Baule waka sran and spirit-spouse figures. Its refined silhouette suggests beauty, composure, and personal presence.
