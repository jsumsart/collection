African Art Record

Fon- or Ewe-Fon-Style Power Figure (Bocio Type)

Fon or Ewe-Fon-style, attribution uncertain

Object Number JA-2018-13
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Fon or Ewe-Fon-style, attribution uncertain
Object Type Power figure / bocio-type composite ceremonial or spiritually significant object

This composite power figure has a carved wooden head and post-like base, cloth-wrapped body, fiber bindings, cowrie shells, cords, and accumulated surface materials. The wrappings and cowries create a dense protective form. Its construction relates to Fon or Ewe-Fon bocio power objects from Benin and neighboring areas.
