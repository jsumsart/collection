African Art Record

Tabwa Musangwe-Style Helmet Mask

Tabwa-style, attribution probable

Object Number JA-2014-11
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Tabwa-style, attribution probable
Object Type Helmet mask / Musangwe-type mask

This helmet-style mask has a rounded dome, facial features carved in relief across a horizontal band, cowrie-shell eyes, incised linear scarification, and lateral attachment perforations. The compact form and cowrie details relate to Tabwa Musangwe-type masks from southeastern Democratic Republic of the Congo. Its low, rounded profile gives the mask a contained, sculptural force.
