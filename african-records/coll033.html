Luba-Style Maternity Figure

Luba-style, attribution uncertain

Object Number JA-2011-22
Date 20th century
Culture / Community Luba-style, attribution uncertain
Object Type Maternity figure / mother-and-child sculpture

This small figural group shows a seated adult supporting or nursing a child. Rounded volumes, a textured coiffure or cap, compact limbs, and an integrated base emphasize closeness and care. The subject evokes continuity, nurture, and lineage within a Luba or related Central African sculptural idiom.
