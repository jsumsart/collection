African Art Record

Seated Colon Figure with Brimmed Hat

West African peoples, possibly Baule or related Cote d Ivoire workshop, attribution uncertain

Object Number JA-2022-30
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community West African peoples, possibly Baule or related Cote d Ivoire workshop, attribution uncertain
Object Type Colon figure / seated genre figure

This carved and painted seated male figure wears a brimmed hat and has simplified facial features, projecting ears, pale painted clothing or body surface, and legs integrated into a chair-like base. The object belongs to the broad African colon-figure genre, which often depicts colonial officials, Europeanized Africans, or contemporary social types in painted wood. Its seated posture and clothing mark it as a social portrait type.
