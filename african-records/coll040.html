African Art Record

Lwalwa Mfondo-Style Face Mask

Lwalwa or Lwalu-style, attribution probable

Object Number JA-2012-12
Date 20th century
Culture / Community Lwalwa or Lwalu-style, attribution probable
Object Type Face mask / Mfondo-type mask

This Lwalwa, or Lwalu, Mfondo-style face mask has an elongated oval form, narrow horizontal eye slits, a small projecting nose, sharply divided red, black, and natural wood-toned pigment, and a V-shaped forehead motif. The angular planes and bold color fields create a highly graphic face. Its form relates closely to Lwalwa/Lwalu masking traditions.
