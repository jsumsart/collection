African Art Record

Lega-Style Lukwakongo Mask with Fiber Beard

Lega peoples, Democratic Republic of the Congo, attribution tentative

Object Number JA-2022-23
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Lega peoples, Democratic Republic of the Congo, attribution tentative
Object Type Lukwakongo-style face mask / Bwami-associated mask

This carved wooden mask has an oval or heart-shaped pale face, long narrow nose, small slit eyes, small mouth, darker brow and border, and attached fiber or raffia beard. These features relate to Lega lukwakongo and Bwami-associated masks from the Democratic Republic of the Congo. The pale face and textured beard create a strong contrast of surface and material.
