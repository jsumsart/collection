Songye Kifwebe-Style Mask with Fiber Beard

Songye-style, attribution probable

Object Number JA-2006-15
Date late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Songye-style, attribution probable
Object Type Face mask / Kifwebe-style mask

This Songye-style kifwebe mask features a tall central crest, narrow eye slits, an elongated striated face, projecting mouth, and plant-fiber beard or costume remnants. The strong linear carving creates a dynamic surface that would have been intensified by movement. Its form belongs to the visual language of kifwebe masquerade in southeastern Central Africa.
