African Art Record

Ngbaka or Ubangi-Region Face Mask

Ngbaka or related Ubangi-region group, attribution uncertain

Object Number JA-2012-13
Date Early–mid 20th century
Culture / Community Ngbaka or related Ubangi-region group, attribution uncertain
Object Type Face mask / masquerade or initiation mask

This elongated wooden mask has narrow slit eyes, a pronounced straight nose, cross-hatched surface decoration, pigment, and attached fiber elements. Its scarification-like patterns and austere face relate to Ngbaka, Mbanza/Banja, and neighboring Ubangi-region masking traditions. The long facial plane creates a formal, solemn presence.
