African Art Record

Bird-Form Figure with Long Beak

Eastern Congolese peoples, possibly Lega or Bembe-related, attribution uncertain

Object Number JA-2011-07
Date 20th century
Culture / Community Eastern Congolese peoples, possibly Lega or Bembe-related, attribution uncertain
Object Type Bird-form figure / zoomorphic sculpture

This carved zoomorphic figure represents a bird with a long downward beak, compact body, reduced limbs, and an inlaid shell eye. The simplified form emphasizes the curve of the beak and the alert point of the eye. Bird imagery appears across several eastern and central Congolese traditions, where animal forms often carry layered social and symbolic meaning.
