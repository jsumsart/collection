African Art Record

Boa-Style Plank Face Mask

Boa-style, attribution uncertain

Object Number JA-2015-03
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Boa-style, attribution uncertain
Object Type Face mask / masquerade mask

This elongated plank-form face mask has a flat reverse, narrow perforated eyes, a pronounced vertical median ridge forming the nose, a rectangular openwork mouth with carved teeth, and abraded white kaolin over dark patina. The stark geometry relates to Boa or related northern Congolese mask traditions. Its pale surface and strong ridge create a striking graphic face.
