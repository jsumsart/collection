African Art Record

Kasai-Region Style Face Mask with Projecting Eyes

Kasai-region or Central African peoples, attribution uncertain

Object Number TC-2015-27
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Kasai-region or Central African peoples, attribution uncertain
Object Type Face mask / masquerade mask

This carved wooden face mask has a rounded form, large projecting circular eyes, long triangular nose, open angular mouth, and painted or incised geometric surface patterning. The features relate to Kasai-region and related Central African masking traditions. Its projecting eyes and patterned surface create a bold, animated face.
