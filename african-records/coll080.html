African Art Record

Yoruba Ceremonial Staff with Figural Finial

Yoruba peoples, attribution probable

Object Number JA-2016-03
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Yoruba peoples, attribution probable
Object Type Ceremonial staff / ritual staff

This Yoruba vertical ceremonial staff is carved from wood and surmounted by a squatting figure holding or supporting a vessel. Stacked geometric and mask-like motifs structure the shaft below. Yoruba carved staffs can be associated with shrine, ceremonial, or prestige contexts, where sculptural form marks spiritual and social significance.
