African Art Record

Bamana Ci Wara-Style Antelope Headdress

Bamana-style, attribution uncertain

Object Number TC-2015-18
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Bamana-style, attribution uncertain
Object Type Headdress / antelope crest / masquerade object

This carved wooden headdress or crest has an arched openwork base, projecting antelope-like forms, a long upward horn-like element, and a small standing figure mounted above the central arch. The antelope and openwork structure relate to Bamana Ci Wara headdresses from Mali. The combination of animal form, human figure, and sweeping vertical line creates a dynamic ceremonial silhouette.
