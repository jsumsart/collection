African Art Record

Songye Nkishi-Style Power Figure

Songye-style, attribution probable

Object Number JA-2014-04
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Songye-style, attribution probable
Object Type Power figure / nkishi or nkisi-style figure

This Songye-style nkisi or nkishi power figure has a faceted mask-like head, metal studs, fiber bindings around the torso, and bifurcated legs. The angular face, applied materials, and binding create a dense visual language of activation and protection. Comparable Songye power figures were understood as effective through added substances, specialist intervention, and social use.
