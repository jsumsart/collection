African Art Record

Kota-Style Reliquary Guardian Figure (Mbulu Ngulu Type)

Kota-style, attribution uncertain

Object Number TC-2015-22
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Kota-style, attribution uncertain
Object Type Reliquary guardian figure / mbulu ngulu-style figure

This wooden reliquary guardian figure has a large oval face, crescent-shaped upper crest, side projections, central neck, and open diamond-shaped lower body. The composition recalls Kota mbulu ngulu reliquary guardian figures from Gabon and the Republic of the Congo. Its gleaming, frontal form evokes the protective presence of ancestral reliquary sculpture.
