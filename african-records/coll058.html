Hemba- or Luba-Hemba-Style Standing Ancestor Figure

Hemba or Luba-Hemba-style, attribution uncertain

Object Number JA-2014-08
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Hemba or Luba-Hemba-style, attribution uncertain
Object Type Ancestor figure / standing figure

This Hemba or Luba-Hemba style ancestor figure has an elongated neck and torso, heart-shaped face, restrained features, chevron scarification on the abdomen, and bead ornaments at the neck and waist. The upright stance and composed expression relate to ancestor-figure traditions in eastern Democratic Republic of the Congo. The figure presents lineage memory through idealized, dignified form.
