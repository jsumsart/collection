African Art Record

Kifwebe-Style Face Mask with Figural Crest

Songye or Luba-style, attribution uncertain

Object Number JA-2022-07
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Songye or Luba-style, attribution uncertain
Object Type Face mask with crest / masquerade mask

This carved wooden face mask has a vertical central ridge, long nose, striated cheek and brow panels, protruding rectangular mouth, and a small figural or crest element above the head. The ridged and striated facial design relates to Songye and Luba kifwebe masking traditions. The crest and projecting mouth add sculptural complexity to the face.
