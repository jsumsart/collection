African Art Record

Ngbandi-Style Face Mask

Ngbandi-style, attribution probable but cautious

Object Number JA-2012-09
Date 20th century
Culture / Community Ngbandi-style, attribution probable but cautious
Object Type Face mask / masquerade mask

This Ngbandi-style wooden mask has an elongated oval form, square eye openings, a pronounced vertical nasal ridge with notched relief, rectangular open mouth with carved teeth, striated cap-like coiffure, and weathered pigment. The severe geometry of the face gives the mask a powerful visual clarity. Its form relates to Ubangi-region masking traditions.
