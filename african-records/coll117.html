African Art Record

Seated Couple Figure

Unidentified Central or West-Central African peoples

Object Number TC-2015-02
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Unidentified Central or West-Central African peoples
Object Type Seated couple figure / figural sculpture

This carved wooden sculpture shows two seated figures joined side by side on a shared base. The figures have simplified rounded heads, incised facial features, compact torsos, bent legs, and hands resting near the knees. The paired format suggests themes of relationship, alliance, lineage, or commemoration.
