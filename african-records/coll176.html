African Art Record

Decorated Calabash Vessel with Coiled Lid

Unidentified African peoples, possibly East African, attribution uncertain

Object Number JA-2022-27
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Unidentified African peoples, possibly East African, attribution uncertain
Object Type Decorated calabash vessel / gourd container

This large calabash or gourd vessel has a rounded lower body, narrow neck, darkened surface, incised shoulder bands, applied light-colored shell or cutout ornaments, and a fitted coiled fiber lid with blue and natural concentric bands. The vessel was likely used or displayed as a container. Its decorated surface and fitted lid give it a refined domestic or ceremonial presence.
