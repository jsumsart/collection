African Art Record

Standing Power Figure with Bindings and Metal Inserts

Central African, possibly Kongo or Teke, attribution uncertain

Object Number JA-2018-19
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Central African, possibly Kongo or Teke, attribution uncertain
Object Type Power figure / ritual figure

This standing human figure has an elongated torso, rounded head, projecting facial features, arms held close to the sides, fiber bindings around the waist, and a vertical row of inserted metal or tack-like elements down the front. The additions and bindings relate to Central African power-object traditions. The figure's surface suggests both adornment and activation.
