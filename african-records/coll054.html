African Art Record

Ngbaka Dagara/Gaza-Style Initiation Mask

Ngbaka-style, attribution probable

Object Number JA-2012-19
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Ngbaka-style, attribution probable
Object Type Face mask / initiation mask / dagara or gaza-type mask

This elongated Ngbaka-style wooden mask has an oval helmet-like form, almond-shaped pierced eyes, projecting straight nose, wide open mouth, heavy patina, and austere carving. The mask's formal severity gives it a commanding presence. Comparable Ngbaka masks from the Ubangi region are associated with initiation and related performance contexts.
