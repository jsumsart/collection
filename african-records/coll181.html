African Art Record

Seated Female Figure with Abdominal Cavity

Central African peoples, possibly Kongo or Teke-related, attribution uncertain

Object Number JA-2022-32
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Central African peoples, possibly Kongo or Teke-related, attribution uncertain
Object Type Seated female figure / figural sculpture

This small seated female figure has a rounded head, raised eyes, open mouth, breasts, hands at the abdomen, bent legs, and a circular cavity or missing inset at the belly. The abdominal cavity relates to Central African figures that held inserted materials, including Kongo or Teke-related power-object traditions. The figure's pose centers attention on the body as a place of meaning.
