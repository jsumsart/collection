African Art Record

Lega-Style Small Face Mask with Raffia Fringe

Lega peoples, Democratic Republic of the Congo, attribution tentative

Object Number JA-2022-15
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Lega peoples, Democratic Republic of the Congo, attribution tentative
Object Type Small face mask / Lega-style maskette

This small carved wooden face mask has a pale facial surface, dark central nose, narrow eyes, red linear markings, small mouth, and surrounding raffia or plant-fiber fringe. The form and fiber surround relate to Lega lukwakongo and related Bwami-associated masks. Its compact face is enlivened by the contrast of pigment and fiber.
