African Art Record

Pair of Kuba-Style Figural Palm-Wine Cups

Kuba or Kuba-influenced Central African peoples, attribution tentative

Object Number JA-2022-34
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Kuba or Kuba-influenced Central African peoples, attribution tentative
Object Type Pair of figural palm-wine cups / prestige cups

This pair of carved wooden cups has deep rounded bowls, pedestal bases, worn polished interiors, and projecting human-head or figural handles. The form relates to Kuba and Kuba-influenced palm-wine or prestige cups from the Democratic Republic of the Congo. The figural handles transform the vessels into objects of status and display.
