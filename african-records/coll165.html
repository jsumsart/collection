African Art Record

Mangbetu-Style Trumbash Sickle Knife

Mangbetu or related northeastern Congo peoples, attribution tentative

Object Number JA-2022-16
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Mangbetu or related northeastern Congo peoples, attribution tentative
Object Type Sickle knife / trumbash-style prestige or ceremonial knife

This curved iron sickle knife or ceremonial blade has a dark forged blade, pierced openings near the blade base, and a carved wooden handle. The crescent-like blade and handle form relate to Mangbetu trumbash or trombash prestige knives from northeastern Democratic Republic of the Congo. The object combines metalwork and carving in a dramatic ceremonial silhouette.
