African Art Record

Pende-Style Mbuya Cap Mask

Pende peoples, Democratic Republic of the Congo, attribution tentative

Object Number JA-2022-38
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Pende peoples, Democratic Republic of the Congo, attribution tentative
Object Type Face mask / mbuya-style cap mask

This carved wooden face mask has a broad rounded forehead, heavy downcast eyelids, long nose, open mouth, projecting ears, pointed chin, and cap-like coiffure with incised crosshatch and horizontal bands. The features relate closely to Pende mbuya and cap-mask traditions from the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Its dramatic face combines refinement with expressive force.
