African Art Record

Standing Figure with Diagonal Sash or Wrapped Torso

Unidentified Central or West-Central African peoples

Object Number JA-2022-33
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Unidentified Central or West-Central African peoples
Object Type Standing figure / figural sculpture

This carved wooden standing figure has an elongated neck, rounded head, dark glossy face, pale highlighted facial features, small ears, and a rectangular upper body crossed by a raised diagonal sash or wrap. The lower body tapers into a long support or skirt-like form. The object relates broadly to Central or West-Central African figural sculpture.
