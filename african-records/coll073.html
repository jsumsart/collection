African Art Record

Kuba Bwoom-Style Helmet Mask

Kuba-style, probable Bwoom-type attribution

Object Number JA-2015-05
Date Not recorded
Culture / Community Kuba-style, probable Bwoom-type attribution
Object Type Helmet mask / Bwoom-type royal mask

This Kuba-style helmet mask closely resembles the Bwoom type, with rounded helmet form, protruding facial features, side flanges, fiber or textile elements, and applied decorative materials. Bwoom is one of the major Kuba royal mask characters. The mask's volume and richly worked surface suggest the theatrical force of Kuba masquerade.
