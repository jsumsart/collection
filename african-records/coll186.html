African Art Record

Dan-Style Face Mask with Fiber Coiffure

Dan-style, Liberia or Cote d Ivoire, attribution tentative

Object Number JA-2022-37
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Dan-style, Liberia or Cote d Ivoire, attribution tentative
Object Type Face mask / masquerade mask

This carved wooden oval mask has a high smooth forehead, large circular eye openings, arched brow ridge, long nose, slightly open protruding mouth, and braided or twisted fiber coiffure along the top and sides. The form relates most closely to Dan-style masks from Liberia and Cote d'Ivoire. Its polished face and fiber coiffure create a vivid contrast of materials.
