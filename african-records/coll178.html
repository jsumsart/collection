African Art Record

Namji-Style Fertility Doll with Bead Ornaments

Namji/Namchi or Dowayo peoples, northern Cameroon, attribution tentative

Object Number JA-2022-29
Date Late 19th–early 20th century
Culture / Community Namji/Namchi or Dowayo peoples, northern Cameroon, attribution tentative
Object Type Fertility doll / decorated figural doll

This carved wooden standing doll-like figure has an elongated geometric body, rounded head, ridged central coiffure or head form, small arms at the sides, projecting navel or abdominal element, and bead ornaments at the ears and neck. The form and embellishment relate strongly to Namji, Namchi, or Dowayo fertility dolls from northern Cameroon. Its ornamented body suggests personal significance and protective presence.
